01

Education SaaS · Offline · Highlight reel

Scholium

A multi-tenant platform for homeschool co-ops, microschools, course providers, and learning centers.

Scholium connects the pieces these organizations have to run: enrollment, attendance, grades, records, progress reports, transcripts, billing, parent access, payments, imports, and the administrative workflows in between. Progress reports are AI-assisted through the Anthropic API.

Security got its own phase, tested live rather than only reviewed: session rotation, login lockout, CSRF rejection, and the authentication flows themselves. Pricing stayed simple, with two flat plans, Co-Op and Learning Center, billed monthly or yearly.

It ran in production for about four months and is offline for now. The highlight reel below walks through it.

Trade & finance SaaS · Offline · Highlight reel

Qorva Global Trade

A workflow platform for solo and small import/export operators.

Qorva covers landed-cost planning from FOB to DDP, shipment tracking, and duty estimates that include Section 301 and 232 additional duties. Around that sit HS-code reference and search, purchase orders, document workflows, reconciliation, reporting, accounting-oriented exports, and live FX rates from frankfurter.app.

Duty estimates are only as good as the tariff data behind them, so the platform keeps its own: a pipeline that tracks the U.S. Harmonized Tariff Schedule revision by revision. Two flat plans, Standard and Pro, billed monthly or yearly.

Qorva reached production and is offline for now. The highlight reel below shows it at work.

03

Financial decision system · Web · Offline

SaaS Financial Decision Suite

Financial decision systems for founder-led SaaS companies, shown end to end on one connected dataset.

The Decision Suite is the flagship demonstration for a planned fixed-scope consulting practice at rosalindgash.com: financial decision systems for founder-led SaaS companies. It is built around Havenpoint Workflow, a SaaS company with 24 months of data.

Note Havenpoint Workflow is fictional, and all of its data is synthetic.

Four connected modules share that one dataset: Performance & Cash, Accounting & Assets, Workforce & Hiring, and Tax Readiness & Expansion, all under an executive overview, the Founder Decision Center. Scenario controls for churn, new-customer pricing, new-business strength, a hire, and UK market entry flow through the 12-month forecast wherever they are financially relevant. A hire changes burn and runway; churn moves ending MRR and cash.

Every formula lives in one calculation library, never inside components, backed by a metric dictionary and an assumptions register. Charts are accessible, with data-table alternatives, as part of WCAG 2.1 AA work. The suite also includes a SaaS Financial Visibility Scorecard and a fail-closed contact form (server action, honeypot). rosalindgash.com is offline for now.

05

Video podcast production · Windows desktop · Personal build · v0.12

RosalindCam Studio

A live-production app for video podcasts, with post-production that stays on the device.

Studio keeps Preview and Program separate, with a deliberate TAKE between them, plus scenes, a run of show, preflight checks, and autosave with recovery. A four-channel mixer (mic, media, system, master) handles mix-minus. It records up to 4K, MKV first and then remuxed to MP4, with seven separate stereo tracks, and streams to YouTube at 1080p30 over RTMPS with an automatic safety recording and bounded reconnects.

A YouTube chat panel feeds a Q&A queue and on-screen comment cards, alongside lower-thirds and ticker graphics. Up to three remote guests join over WebRTC, through a green room and private join links.

Post-production runs on the device, with no cloud and no per-use fees: dialogue isolation; a transcript editor built on Whisper; an episode producer that drafts titles, descriptions, chapters, and show notes with inspectable citations, using a local LLM through llama.cpp; clip drafts with 9:16 framing and captions; and thumbnail concepts from a local image model. I review everything before it is exported.

Studio shares a Core library with RosalindCam and comes with a 22-page printable user guide. It is a personal build, and real-device acceptance of the guest features is still in progress.

06

Screen & webcam recorder · Windows desktop · Personal build

RosalindCam

A focused recorder for talking-head and screen videos.

RosalindCam records screen only, webcam only, or screen and webcam together, with microphone audio, to 1080p MP4. The webcam can sit side by side with the screen or float as a movable, resizable picture-in-picture that can be adjusted while recording.

I wanted to hide my room on camera without a green screen, so it does real-time background replacement (a colour or an image) or blur, using the RobustVideoMatting model on the GPU through ONNX Runtime and DirectML, with a CPU fallback and edge controls.

Recording is crash-safe: it writes MKV first, then remuxes to MP4, using AMD hardware H.264 with a software fallback. It ships as a portable, self-contained .exe, backed by a 99-check native smoke suite.

How I work

Six principles, drawn from the work above.

Not aspirations: each one shows up somewhere in the seven chapters above.

  1. 1

    Start from the problem, not the ticket.

    The underlying problem decides what gets built. Features follow from it, not the other way round.

  2. 2

    Own the whole stack.

    Product decisions, UX, architecture, implementation, payments, deployment, and hardening, with one person answerable for all of it.

  3. 3

    AI is a tool; accountability is mine.

    I use AI as an engineering tool. I delegate architecture and implementation, but responsibility for requirements, review, verification, security, and final product decisions stays with me.

  4. 4

    Verify live, not just on paper.

    Static review is the start. Scholium’s security phase tested session rotation, lockout, and CSRF rejection against the running app; the Decision Suite runs 49 automated validation checks.

  5. 5

    Right-size the solution.

    Five CSV exports replaced two planned accounting integrations: less code to maintain, and compatible with any accounting tool.

  6. 6

    Build the tool I need.

    I have more time than money. RosalindCam and Studio exist because building my own was more cost-efficient than paying for someone else’s.
