01Education SaaS

Scholium

A multi-tenant platform for homeschool co‑ops, microschools, course providers, and learning centers.

What I built

  • One system connecting enrollment, attendance, grades, records, progress reports, transcripts, billing, parent access, payments, imports, and administrative workflows.
  • AI-assisted progress reports through the Anthropic API.
  • Five CSV export types.
  • Two plans, Co-Op and Learning Center, at flat monthly or yearly pricing.

Notable decisions

Rolled back the accounting integrations. I removed planned Xero and QuickBooks integrations, 13 files and 4 database tables, in favor of comprehensive CSV exports. Simpler, and it works with any accounting tool.

A dedicated security phase, tested live. Session rotation, login lockout, CSRF rejection, and auth flows were verified against the running app.

02Trade & finance SaaS

Qorva Global Trade

A workflow platform for solo and small import/export operators.

What I built

  • Landed-cost planning from FOB to DDP, with duty estimates that include Section 301 and 232 additional duties.
  • Shipment tracking, HS-code reference and search, purchase orders, and document workflows.
  • Reconciliation, reporting, and accounting-oriented exports.
  • Live FX rates through a REST integration with frankfurter.app.
  • Two plans, Standard and Pro, at flat monthly or yearly pricing.

Notable decisions

A tariff data pipeline instead of static rates. It fetches each new revision of the U.S. Harmonized Tariff Schedule, diffs it against the prior revision, and updates base rates, Chapter 99 additional duties, and preference-program data to match.

03Financial decision system · Web

SaaS Financial Decision Suite

The flagship demonstration for a planned fixed-scope consulting practice at rosalindgash.com: financial decision systems for founder-led SaaS companies.

It runs on Havenpoint Workflow, a fictional SaaS company with 24 months of synthetic data. The company and its data are not real.

What I built

  • Four connected modules on one dataset: Performance & Cash, Accounting & Assets, Workforce & Hiring, and Tax Readiness & Expansion, plus an executive overview, the Founder Decision Center.
  • Scenario controls for churn, new-customer pricing, new-business strength, a hire, and UK market entry. They flow through the 12-month forecast where they are financially relevant: a hire changes burn and runway; churn moves ending MRR and cash.
  • Accessible charts with data-table alternatives (WCAG 2.1 AA work), a SaaS Financial Visibility Scorecard, and a fail-closed contact form (server action, honeypot).

Notable decisions

49 automated validation checks. A 24-month MRR bridge, cash reconciliation, assets = liabilities + equity, NPV/IRR recomputation, a book-to-tax bridge, and scenario propagation.

One calculation library. Every formula lives in a central library and is never computed in components, backed by a metric dictionary and an assumptions register.

Live production

  • Separate Preview and Program with a deliberate TAKE; scenes, run of show, preflight checks, autosave, and recovery.
  • A four-channel audio mixer (mic, media, system, master) with mix-minus.
  • Records up to 4K (MKV first, remuxed to MP4) with seven separate stereo tracks. Streams to YouTube at 1080p30 over RTMPS with an automatic safety recording and bounded reconnects.
  • A YouTube chat panel with a Q&A queue and on-screen comment cards; lower-thirds and ticker graphics.
  • Up to three remote guests over WebRTC, with a green room and private join links.

On-device post-production

No cloud, no per-use fees. Dialogue isolation; a transcript editor (Whisper); an episode producer that drafts titles, descriptions, chapters, and show notes with inspectable citations (local LLM via llama.cpp); clip drafts with 9:16 framing and captions; and thumbnail concepts from a local image model.

Everything is reviewed before export. The local models draft; I check and approve what ships.

06Screen & webcam recorder · Windows desktop

RosalindCam

A focused recorder for talking-head and screen videos.

What I built

  • Records screen only, webcam only, or screen + webcam, with microphone audio, to 1080p MP4. The webcam can be a movable, resizable picture-in-picture (adjustable while recording) or side by side.
  • Real-time background replacement (color or image) or blur without a green screen, using the RobustVideoMatting model on the GPU (ONNX Runtime + DirectML, CPU fallback), with edge controls.

Notable decisions

Crash-safe recording. It writes MKV first, then remuxes to MP4, using AMD hardware H.264 with a software fallback.

A portable, self-contained .exe. Backed by a 99-check native smoke suite.

How I work

Six principles behind the work.

Grounded in how the projects above were actually built.

  1. Start from the problem, not the ticket.

    I begin with what the people running the operation actually have to get done, then decide what the software should be.

  2. Own the whole stack.

    Product decisions, UX, architecture, implementation, payments, deployment, and hardening sit with one accountable owner: me.

  3. AI is a tool; accountability is mine.

    I use AI as an engineering tool. I delegate architecture and implementation, but responsibility for requirements, review, verification, security, and final product decisions stays with me.

  4. Verify live, not just on paper.

    Scholium's security phase was tested against the running app. The Decision Suite carries 49 validation checks; RosalindCam Studio, 837.

  5. Right-size the solution.

    Scholium dropped 13 files and 4 tables of planned accounting integrations for CSV exports that work with any accounting tool.

  6. Build the tool I need.

    I have more time than money. When the tools on offer don't fit, I build my own, which is how both RosalindCam apps started.
